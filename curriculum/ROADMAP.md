## Competency ladder

1. **AI-assisted developer:** prompts, inspects, and accepts results.
2. **AI workflow engineer:** controls context, tools, approvals, tests and reproducibility.
3. **AI engineering architect:** designs evaluation, security boundaries, orchestration, economics and team standards.

## Assessment principles

Learners submit evidence: configuration diff, command transcript, test results, quality rubric, cost observations, and rationale. Claims of improvements must be supported by repeated trials, not single anecdotal runs.

## Publication gates

- Technical review by a practitioner
- Vendor-feature verification and date-stamp
- Student-run reproducibility check
- Security and intellectual-property review
- Instructional review: outcomes and rubric are aligned
