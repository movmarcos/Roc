# Facilitator guide — Lesson 01 / Lab 01

**Learning trap:** experienced practitioners often use “memory” to describe any apparently consistent assistant response. Do not dismiss their experience; convert it into falsifiable hypotheses.

## Facilitation flow
1. Ask learners to predict which of three repository facts will survive a fresh session (5 min).
2. Present the five-layer mental model (10 min).
3. Run or assign the four-trial controlled lab (60–90 min).
4. Compare expected and observed results; identify confounders (15 min).
5. Debrief: what belongs in explicit versioned knowledge versus on-demand retrieval? (10 min).

## Common confounders
- The “fresh” session retained platform-specific project state.
- The model used a repo search, not previously learned knowledge.
- The question accidentally gave away its answer.
- A guide was changed along with code, confounding results.
- Token usage numbers differ in scope by product and subscription.

## Evidence of mastery
The learner can explain where a claim came from, reproduce observations, challenge the inference, and propose a better controlled experiment.

## Publishing tasks
Before sale, record demo footage, verify current product commands, add screenshots with rights clearance, and pilot the lab with at least two external engineers.
