# Module 01 assessment rubric

| Dimension | 0 — Missing | 1 — Partial | 2 — Competent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mental model | Conflates training and context | Names differences | Explains model, context, files and tool observation clearly |
| Experiment design | Uncontrolled prompts | Partial controls | Comparable sessions, ground truth, version/settings recorded |
| Evidence | Relies on impressions | Some path evidence | Checks claims against source and records uncertainty |
| Trade-offs | No recommendation | Generic recommendation | Specific, bounded context/storage policy with caveats |

**Passing:** 6/8 or better, with nonzero evidence score. A higher score should require reproducible artifact quality, not vocabulary recall.
