# Commercial product strategy — working draft

## Intended customer
Experienced developers, data engineers, solution architects, engineering leads and technical teams already using AI coding assistants.

## Differentiation
A technically rigorous, vendor-neutral course built around reproducible experiments and measured engineering outcomes instead of introductory prompting.

## Product formats to validate
- Self-paced course with downloadable labs
- Instructor-led advanced workshop
- Team / enterprise cohort training
- Companion handbook and template pack

## Proof before pricing
Pilot with advanced practitioners, collect completion and outcome metrics, test willingness to pay, and identify where proprietary product features create maintenance burden. Do not assert market demand before validation.

## Intellectual property
Create original lessons and diagrams. Attribute external sources and verify any third-party code, screenshot, logo, or excerpt rights. Keep student and instructor assets separate before packaging.
