# Product brief — draft

**Working title:** AI Engineering Mastery — Beyond Prompting  
**Promise:** Move from productive AI-assisted coding to engineered, observable, reliable and cost-aware AI development workflows.

**Primary audience:** experienced developers, senior data engineers, solution architects and technical leads using AI coding assistants in real repositories.

**Differentiator:** a vendor-neutral engineering methodology demonstrated in Claude Code, Codex and GitHub Copilot, with measurable labs and reusable implementation assets.

**Potential formats:** self-paced course; cohort-based lab programme; company workshop; technical handbook.

**Commercial assumptions to validate:** willingness to pay, ideal course length, buyer personas, operational cost of labs, software licensing burden, and demand for vendor comparison versus deep single-tool specialisation.

**Proof required before selling:** run a pilot cohort, collect structured results, conduct legal/IP review of examples, verify version-sensitive demonstrations and produce accessibility-friendly subtitles and transcripts.

**Do not promise:** guaranteed savings, universal agent autonomy, or permanent compatibility with vendor features.
